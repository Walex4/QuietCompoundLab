# Where does your income really land?

A free calculator from [Quiet Compound](https://www.youtube.com/@QuietCompoundLab).

Enter your household income, state and household size to see:

- your income class (Pew Research Center method), nationally and adjusted for prices in your state
- where you rank among US households
- what you actually keep after 2026 federal tax, Social Security, Medicare and an estimated state tax
- your real federal tax rate versus your bracket

Every number links to its source on the page (IRS, Census Bureau, SSA, BEA, Pew Research Center).
Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

For education, not financial advice.
