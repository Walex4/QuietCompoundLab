Sources. Tax brackets, standard deductions and the $24,500 401(k) limit: IRS 2026 figures. Social Security wage base $184,500: SSA. Middle-class range: Pew Research Center method (two-thirds to double the median, adjusted for household size) on Census ACS 2024 data, scaled by BEA Regional Price Parities (2023, released Dec 2024) for your state. US rank: household income percentiles from Census CPS ASEC 2026 (2025 incomes) via DQYDJ. State tax: each state's tax on a $100,000 single salary in 2026 (PayBrackets), applied as a flat rate, so treat it as an estimate.
